Lost your authenticator?

If you've lost or replaced your authenticator device, we can reset your multi-factor authentication.

Here's what happens:

  1. Contact support at support@doctorhide.com and verify your account ownership
  2. An administrator will reset your MFA device and issue new backup codes
  3. You'll set up a new authenticator on your device

For security, we require you to verify your identity before resetting MFA.
